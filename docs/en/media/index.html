February 4, 2026: NTT Data Institute of Management Consulting (Hirakawacho, Tokyo)

Hamada spoke on “Machine neuroscience with neuro-aligned AI: application to digital twins through persona simulation”. Post Website

Recent advances show that generative AI models can accurately predict neural responses, opening up many uses for functional models of the brain. This talk introduced “neuro-aligned AI”, which aligns AI systems with the brain’s representations and dynamics and with human behavior. It discussed how to dissect and interpret modern AI systems with neuroscientific methods such as causal analysis, representational geometry, and circuit intervention. By treating AI as a model of the brain, it proposed a framework for machine neuroscience that bridges advanced AI systems and the human nervous system, and discussed the future of AI agents working with the persona simulator under development.
