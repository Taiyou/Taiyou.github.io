About

I am a neuroscientist working on the interpretability and alignment of large language models. I received my Ph.D. from the Neural Computation Unit at the Okinawa Institute of Science and Technology (OIST), supervised by Prof. Kenji Doya, where I showed that activating serotonin neurons drives brain-wide activity in awake mice (Nature Communications, 2024). At Araya Inc. in Tokyo, where I have worked since August 2019, I lead Persona Lab, which applies the methods of neuroscience to LLMs: where psychological concepts are represented, how intervening on them shifts behavior, and how concept networks can inform alignment.

Alongside research, I convene conversations on how society should adapt to advanced AI. I directed Funding the Commons Tokyo, featuring Audrey Tang and Glen Weyl, and run the DeSci Tokyo community.
