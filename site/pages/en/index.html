About

I study the neural mechanisms of curiosity at Araya Inc. in Tokyo, where I have worked since August 2019. I received my Ph.D. from the Neural Computation Unit at the Okinawa Institute of Science and Technology (OIST), supervised by Prof. Kenji Doya.

Research fields
Systems neuroscience, cognitive neuroscience, neurophenomenology, positive psychology
Topics
Serotonin, brain-wide computation, curiosity, consciousness, open science
Current projects

2026 – 2027

DeSci Tokyo interview series

A video series in which I interview Prof. Genki Kanda (Institute of Science Tokyo), Brandon Possin (Merito Network), and Dr. Ken Suzuki (natural philosopher) on research automation, research funding, and science and society in the age of AI. Six full episodes and 53 trailers and shorts, releasing from November 10, 2026.

2024

Funding the Commons Tokyo

Speakers included Audrey Tang, Glen Weyl, and Tokyo Governor Yuriko Koike. Two days at United Nations University with 304 participants. I directed the planning and operations.

2023 –

DeSci Tokyo

A community hub supporting researchers and research organizations through decentralized systems. Its launch crowdfunding raised about 2.35 million yen and reached its goal in a platform-record 5 hours 43 minutes.

2026

PRANCE International Symposium: Reverse Alignment

With Glen Weyl as special speaker, the symposium asked whether a plural society can accept AI, and how society itself must change.

2019 – 2025

Consciousness Club Tokyo

A Tokyo hub for consciousness research. I ran 29 talks with speakers such as Max Hodak (former Neuralink CEO), growing registrations from 33 to over 2,000.

2024

DAO UTokyo

Co-hosted with the Ethereum Foundation at the University of Tokyo’s Fukutake Hall, discussing research, law, and policy for DAOs and Web3. I was a member of the planning team.

2022 – 2024

metascientia

Persona Lab

Persona Lab

Since its launch in November 2025, I have led Persona Lab as team leader, from research on LLM internals to social simulation and a platform where people and AIs work together.

Research

Psychological concepts inside LLMs

Analyzing LLMs with the methods used to study the brain: where psychological concepts are represented, what changes when they are manipulated, and how this can inform alignment.

Social simulation

Predicting opinion distributions with AI personas

Led to joint research on an “AI panel” with NTT DATA Institute of Management Consulting and selection for ATLA’s Social Simulation Project.

Product

ClanExe

A platform where people and AIs work as a team. I led its development from the product site to the full release in about four months.

2025 –

Building and leading the team
