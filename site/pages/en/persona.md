## My role

- **Research**: Started a research line that analyzes the internal representations of LLMs with neuroscience methods, and lead work on where psychological concepts are represented in a model and what changes when they are manipulated.
- **Product**: Led the development of the AI partner platform ClanExe, from the product site (June 2026) through the closed beta (July) to the full release (October) in about four months.

Persona Lab is a research organization in the NeuroAI division of Araya Inc. [[Team page](https://lp.araya.org/neuroai/neurotech/persona-lab)]

## Research: psychological concepts inside LLMs

I apply the methods neuroscience uses to study the brain (decoding, probing, and manipulating neural activity) to large language models, asking where and how psychological concepts such as personality and values are represented inside a model. What happens to probing results and generation when concept neurons are manipulated? Can treating psychological concepts as a network give us a handle on aligning AI behavior with human values? The strength of this approach is that these questions can be tested with the same tools used on brains. See also [Research](/en/research/).

## Social simulation: predicting opinion distributions with AI personas

The team develops an “AI panel” that asks a group of AI personas with different backgrounds the same question and examines the distribution of answers and the reasons behind them. It is a technology for estimating, in advance, how groups that surveys rarely reach will respond, and how information will be received in society. The answers are generated by AI and are not those of real people.

- Joint research on the AI panel with NTT DATA Institute of Management Consulting, combined with its Human Information Database®, on consumer agents and the prevention of financial fraud. (August 2026)
- Selected for ATLA’s Social Simulation Project, for research on improving the prediction of how opinions are distributed in society. (December 2025)

## People and AIs as a team: ClanExe

[ClanExe](https://clanexe.araya.org/) is a platform for creating AIs with their own personalities, values, skills, and knowledge, so that people and AIs can work as a team. It offers multi-agent collaboration, in which AIs with different roles divide up research, writing, and review, along with the AI panel. How knowledge and decisions are handed over and governed when many AIs and people work as one organization is also the theme of my essay on machine organizations.

- Hamada, H.T. (2026) Machine Organization with Knowledge Management: New Developments in Knowledge Creation Management through AI and Process Management. Oyo Buturi 95(8), 449-452. (in Japanese) [[Paper](https://doi.org/10.11470/oubutsu.95.8_449)] [[Announcement](https://www.araya.org/publications/media20260805/)]
