## About Persona Lab

Persona Lab is Araya’s NeuroAI research organization. It aims at the next generation of consumer understanding through digital twin technology that incorporates personality information, and works on both basic research, which analyzes the internal representations of large language models (LLMs) with neuroscience methods, and its business applications. It was launched in November 2025. [[Team page](https://lp.araya.org/neuroai/neurotech/persona-lab)]

## ClanExe

[ClanExe](https://clanexe.araya.org/) is an AI partner platform for creating AIs with their own personalities, values, skills, and knowledge, and working with them as a team. It is developed by Persona Lab, and the full version became available on October 21, 2026.

- **Character make**: Create AI members by combining personality, speaking style, skills, and knowledge.
- **Multi-agent collaboration**: AIs with different roles, such as research, writing, and review, divide up the work.
- **AI panel / social simulation**: Ask a group of AI personas with different backgrounds the same question, then organize the distribution of answers and the reasons behind them. The answers are generated by AI and are not those of real customers.
