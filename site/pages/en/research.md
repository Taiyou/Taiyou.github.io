## Serotonin regulation of brain-wide dynamics [Hamada et al., Nat Commun, 2024]

![Brain-wide activation map, session 1 (awake)](serotonin-1.png)
![Results of serotonin neuron stimulation](serotonin-2.png)

We investigated the brain-wide response to optogenetic stimulation of dorsal raphe serotonin neurons in awake mice with 11.7T high-resolution MRI.

### Findings
- We observed a brain-wide positive BOLD response, including reward-related circuits, which was replicated in another session.
- We observed a brain-wide negative response under an anesthetized state, consistent with a previous study, while the positive response persisted in the awake state.
- The brain-wide BOLD response showed an association with the structural density of dorsal raphe serotonin projections.
- Using mRNA maps of 5-HT receptors and structural density, we fit them to the brain-wide response to optogenetic stimulation. The inhibitory 5-HT1 system showed negative weights while the excitatory 5-HT2 system showed positive weights. Furthermore, the weights of the receptor systems may differ between anesthetized and awake states.

We will study how the serotonin system contributes to brain dynamics and cognitive tasks.
